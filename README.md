## ⚡ Core Modules & Architecture

### 1. Main Executive Dashboard
- **Daily Transformation Score**: Dynamic 0–100 index weighted across Gym, Protein, Hydration, Meditation, Sleep, and Habits.
- **Apple Fitness-Style Activity Rings**: Triple concentric animated SVG rings (Move, Mind, Fuel).
- **Start Today Action Engine**: One-click quick launcher into today's prescribed training split.
- **Adaptive Timeline**: Real-time checklist synced with daily office and gym schedule.

### 2. Intelligent Gym Trainer Mode
- **Split Library**:
  - Monday: Chest & Triceps Hypertrophy
  - Tuesday: Back & Biceps Thickness
  - Wednesday: Leg Foundation & Core Strength
  - Thursday: Active Recovery & Cardio
  - Friday: Shoulder Cannon & Upper Power
  - Saturday: Athletic Conditioning & Sports
  - Sunday: Mind Master & Total Regeneration
- **13+ Anatomically Modelled Exercises**: Dumbbell Chest Press, Incline DB Press, Lat Pulldown, Cable Row, 45° Leg Press, Squats, Shoulder Press, Bicep Curls, Tricep Extensions, Romanian Deadlifts, Planks, Incline Treadmill, Elliptical.
- **Interactive Execution Screen**:
  - 60px+ high-contrast touch targets for wet gym hands.
  - Automatic Rest Countdown timer with Web Audio synthesised chimes.
  - Progressive Overload Advisor: Prompts +1.25kg / +2.5kg only when all prescribed reps are met with clean form.
  - Pain / Discomfort Reporting: Instant stop protocol and safe exercise substitutions.

### 3. GPS Running & Cardio Hub
- **Interactive Leaflet + OpenStreetMap Engine**: Live polyline route tracking, pace (min/km), moving speed, and caloric burn.
- **Desk Test Simulator**: Realistic GPS route generator for testing without faking athlete data.
- **Progressive Distance Ladders**: 1K, 3K, 5K, and 10K road ladders.

### 4. Swimming & Cycling Modes
- **Swimming Tracker**: Pool 25m / 50m and Open-Water tracking, giant one-tap lap button, stroke selector (Freestyle, Breaststroke, Backstroke, Butterfly, Mixed), and SWOLF pace calculator.
- **Aquatic Safety**: Zero underwater hypoxia games.
- **GPS Cycling**: Moving time, max speed, and elevation tracker.

### 5. Mind & Meditation OS
- **Animated Breathing Circle**: 4-4 Calm, 4-4-4-4 Navy SEAL Box Breathing, and 4-7-8 Deep Sleep Decompression.
